⚠️ **Do NOT reapply to AdSense until:**
1. You have 30-40 substantial (1500+) word articles
2. Site gets consistent organic traffic (5K+/month)
3. Average content quality dramatically improved
4. You've been publishing consistently for 2+ months

⚠️ **Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- Reapplying too quickly (wait minimum 4-6 weeks)
- AI-generated content without human editing
- Duplicate content from other sites
- Thin affiliate content
- Clickbait headlines without substance
- Too many calculator pages vs educational content
